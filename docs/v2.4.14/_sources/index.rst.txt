SuPyMode documentation
======================

Model optical supermodes in tapered fiber components. Build a cross-section, solve the modes along a taper, and inspect effective indices, coupling and propagation.

Before you interpret results
----------------------------

Mesh resolution, taper sampling and boundary symmetries affect mode tracking.
Check convergence before interpreting coupling or propagation. See :doc:`theory`.
